## PROJECTS

### AI Resume Parser & Job Description Analyzer | 2026
**Python — FastAPI — LLM — NLP — PyPDF — python-docx — Pydantic**
- Built an AI-powered resume analysis system that extracts and structures candidate information from **PDF and DOCX resumes** using automated document-processing pipelines.
- Developed a **FastAPI REST backend** with Pydantic schemas to convert unstructured resume and job-description content into structured candidate profiles.
- Implemented **skill matching and weighted evaluation** to compare candidate profiles against job descriptions and generate a **0–100 suitability score**.
- Integrated the system into an AI portfolio application, enabling conversational analysis of resumes, projects, skills, and job-specific requirements.
